# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Critique ideas and artefacts, not people. Welcome questions, admit uncertainty, preserve negative results and make space for correction.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information or credentials, manipulation of research results, plagiarism, licence violations and attempts to bypass safety/compliance controls.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled proportionately, confidentially and without retaliation. The owner may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect contributors, users and research integrity.
